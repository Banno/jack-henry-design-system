# Caption

<hr />

`<forge-caption>` is a text primitive for supporting text that's smaller and quieter than the main text around it, such as helper text under a form field, a footnote, or a label under an image.

## Usage

Import the component:

```javascript
import '@jack-henry/jh-elements/components/caption/caption.js';
```

To use `<forge-caption>`:

```html
<forge-caption>We'll never share your email with anyone else.</forge-caption>
```

## Accessibility

This component satisfies [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) AA success criteria.
The following success criteria are of concern:

- [WCAG 1.3.1 Info and Relationships](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/info-and-relationships.html): Information, structure, and relationships conveyed through presentation can be programmatically determined or are available in text.

- [WCAG 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html): The visual presentation of text and images of text has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1.

- [WCAG 1.4.4 Resize Text](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/resize-text.html): Except for captions and images of text, text can be resized without assistive technology up to 200 percent without loss of content or functionality.

### What we provide

- To satisfy [WCAG 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), the default secondary text color meets the minimum contrast of 4.5:1.

- To satisfy [WCAG 1.4.4 Resize Text](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/resize-text.html), `<forge-caption>` can be resized without assistive technology up to 200 percent without loss of content or functionality.

### Author Guidance

- To satisfy [WCAG 1.3.1 Info and Relationships](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/info-and-relationships.html) when using `<forge-caption>` as helper, error, or descriptive text for another control, associate the two by setting `aria-describedby` (or `aria-labelledby`, as appropriate) on the related control to the caption's `id`.
