# Primitives

Primitives are low-level, unopinionated building blocks. They carry no visual
styling of their own — their job is to arrange other components.

## Layout primitives

- **[Column](?path=/docs/primitives-column--docs)** — arranges
  its children in a vertical column (`display: flex; flex-direction: column`).
- **[Row](?path=/docs/primitives-row--docs)** — arranges
  its children in a horizontal row (`display: flex; flex-direction: row`).

Both expose `gap` and `padding` (mapped to dimension tokens) and
`align-x`, `align-y`, and `wrap` (mapped to flexbox).

## Seeing them in action

Individually these primitives don't mean much — they're designed to compose,
and a stack is itself a valid flex child, so they nest freely. See
**[Layout Composition](?path=/docs/primitives-layout-composition--docs)** for
real-world patterns: toolbars, nested-stack cards, form rows, and wrapping.
