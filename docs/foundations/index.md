# Foundations

Foundations are the building blocks underneath every Forge component: the colors, type, spacing, depth, and shapes that give the design system its personality, plus the voice and tone that carry that personality into words. Get these right, and everything built on top of them — buttons, badges, tables, whole pages — feels consistent without anyone having to think about it.

This section is still growing alongside the rest of Forge 2.0. Here's what's available today, and what's on the way.

## What's here

- **[Colors](./colors.md)** — the global color palette, alpha tokens, and the alias system that keeps color usage consistent and accessible across products.
- **[Typography](./typography.md)** — our font families and type scale, and how to choose the right style for the job.
- **[Voice and tone](./voice-and-tone.md)** — how we sound, and how that shifts depending on context.

## Coming soon

- **Layout** — spacing, grids, and how content should breathe on the page.
- **Elevation & depth** — how we use shadow and layering to show hierarchy.
- **Shapes** — corner radii and the shape language behind our components.
- **Do's and don'ts** — general usage guidance that spans the whole system.

We'll fill these in as that guidance is finalized. If you need something that isn't documented yet, reach out on our [GitHub discussion board](https://github.com/Banno/jack-henry-design-system/discussions).
