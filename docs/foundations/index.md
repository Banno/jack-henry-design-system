# Foundations

Foundations are the building blocks underneath every Forge component: the design tokens and terminology, colors, type, layout, depth, shapes, and iconography that give the design system its personality, plus the voice and tone that carry that personality into words, and the accessibility principles that hold it all together. Get these right, and everything built on top of them — buttons, badges, tables, whole pages — feels consistent without anyone having to think about it.

## What's here

- **[Overview](./overview.md)** — key terminology and the design-token conventions that apply across every foundation and component.
- **[Colors](./colors.md)** — the global color palette, alpha tokens, and the alias system that keeps color usage consistent and accessible across products.
- **[Typography](./typography.md)** — our font families and type scale, and how to choose the right style for the job.
- **[Layout](./layout.md)** — the dimension scale that keeps spacing and sizing consistent across every surface.
- **[Elevation & depth](./elevation-and-depth.md)** — how we use shadow and z-index to show hierarchy and layering.
- **[Shapes](./shapes.md)** — corner radius, border concepts, and the focus ring that give components their shape and boundaries.
- **[Icons](./icons.md)** — naming, style, sizing, and color guidance for Forge iconography.
- **[Voice and tone](./voice-and-tone.md)** — how we sound, and how that shifts depending on context.
- **[Accessibility](./accessibility.md)** — the standards, testing practices, and documentation requirements that keep Forge usable by everyone.
